\sect{Part C: marking guide}{20 marks}
\textbf{C1 memo (10).} Content 4, format 3, language 3.
Format: ``MEMORANDUM'', To / From / Date / Subject lines, short body, name and designation, no salutation or closing.
Must contain: what (file server unavailable), when (Saturday 12 December, 9 pm to 6 am), why (disk replacement and OS patching),
impact (no access to home directories and shared files), action (save and close work, copy urgent files locally before 9 pm), contact (name and extension).
Deduct for a letter-style layout, missing date or subject, or more than about 150 words.

then backup of student data and disk-health (SMART) checks, then replacement of failing units on a priority list, then a review after the next monsoon.
