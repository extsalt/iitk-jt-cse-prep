\sect{Part C: what the examiner looks for}
\textbf{C1 Official letter.} Sender's designation and department; date; ``To, The Head, Department of CSE'';
a one-line subject (``Request for purchase of 10 UPS units for the computer lab''); salutation; body in three short
paragraphs (the problem with dates, the request with quantity, the benefit); ``Yours faithfully''; name and designation.
Formal tone, no contractions.

\textbf{C2 Incident report.} Title; date, time and location; reported by; then headed sections:
\emph{Summary}, \emph{Timeline}, \emph{Impact} (which services, how long), \emph{Cause}, \emph{Action taken},
\emph{Preventive measures} (temperature alarm, backup cooling, automatic alerts, a weekend on-call contact).
Past tense, facts only, no blame.

\textbf{Self-marking out of 10 each:} format 3, content 4, language 3.
